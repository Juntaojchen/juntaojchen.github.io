## Place Notes

- City: Chongqing, China
- Coordinates: 29.5630° N, 106.5516° E
- Scene: Night skyline by the river

## My Reflection

Standing by the river at night, the city felt like a moving circuit board: light, flow, and rhythm.

The bridge lines and dense buildings made me think about scale. From far away, it looks ordered; up close, every street has its own uncertainty and noise. It is a good reminder that both research and life often work the same way.
